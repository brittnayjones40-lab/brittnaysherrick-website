A Story Behind the Stories

The Worlds Came First

Growing up in Michigan, I spent autumns working at the cider mill and wandering the woods behind it.

Every hill became a kingdom. Every trail led somewhere unknown.

For years, those worlds lived only in notebooks, journal pages, and daydreams.

The Secret

The truth is, I never thought I could be a writer.

Writing was something other people did.

People with talent. People with experience. People who belonged in publishing.

The Turning Point

But stories have a way of refusing to leave you alone.

One night while traveling for work, I opened my laptop and made a simple promise to myself:

Just try.

I wrote a chapter.
Then another.
Then another.

What Writing Taught Me

What began as an act of courage became a calling.

Courage isn't the absence of fear.

It's choosing to take the first step before you're ready.

Today, those childhood dreams have become The Shards of Starlight.

“Courage isn't the absence of fear.
It's choosing to take the first step before you're ready.”
The belief behind every story I tell.

Why I Write

I write because I believe anything is possible if you're willing to try.

I believe in hard work. I believe in learning through failure. I believe in ordinary people discovering they're stronger than they ever imagined.

And I love the moment a story comes alive. When a character faces an impossible choice. When a world unfolds beyond what I thought was possible.

That's the magic that keeps me coming back to the page.

Official Bio

Brittnay Sherrick is an epic fantasy author from Michigan and creator of The Shards of Starlight, a sweeping fantasy series inspired by childhood adventures, found family, and the courage to choose hope.

After reconnecting with a love of fantasy fiction as an adult, she began writing the worlds and stories she had imagined for years.

She lives in Michigan with her family and a lively household of beloved pets.
