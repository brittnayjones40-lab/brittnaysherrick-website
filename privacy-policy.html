Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

1. About This Policy

Brittnay Sherrick ("Brittnay," "I," "me," or "my") operates the website brittnaysherrick.com (the "Site").

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information may be collected when someone visits the Site, joins the Starlight Society mailing list, or contacts me. It also explains how that information is used, shared, stored, and managed.

By providing personal information through the Site, visitors acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information I Collect

Information provided directly

Starlight Society subscriptions

When someone joins the Starlight Society, the following information may be collected:

  • First name
  • Email address
  • Signup and subscription status
  • Any communication preferences voluntarily provided

The subscription form is used to deliver Letters from the Starlight, including book news, writing updates, early reveals, behind-the-scenes material, and occasional reader opportunities.

Direct communications

When someone contacts me by email or through another available communication method, I may receive:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Message content
  • Any other information voluntarily included in the message

Please do not send sensitive personal information through ordinary email.

Information processed automatically

When someone visits the Site, the hosting provider may process limited technical information needed to deliver, protect, and operate the Site. This information may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser and device type
  • Operating system
  • Date and time of access
  • Requested page or file
  • Referring website
  • Technical or security-related event information

The Site is hosted through Vercel. Vercel describes itself as a frontend cloud used to deploy and operate websites and explains that it may process information at a customer's direction, with the customer acting as the controller of that information.

3. How Information Is Used

Personal information may be used to:

  • Deliver Letters from the Starlight to confirmed subscribers
  • Send subscription and confirmation messages
  • Provide book news, writing updates, early reveals, and related communications
  • Respond to questions, reader messages, and professional inquiries
  • Maintain subscription preferences and process unsubscribe requests
  • Operate, maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the Site
  • Prevent spam, fraud, misuse, or unauthorized activity
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations
  • Establish, exercise, or defend legal rights when necessary

Personal information will not be used for purposes materially different from those described here without an appropriate notice or permission.

4. Legal Bases for Processing

Where the GDPR, UK GDPR, or similar laws apply, personal information is processed under the following legal bases:

  • Consent: Newsletter subscription information is processed based on the subscriber's consent.
  • Legitimate interests: Limited technical information may be processed to operate, protect, maintain, and improve the Site, provided those interests do not override an individual's rights.
  • Responding to a request: Contact information and message content are processed to answer a message or take steps requested by the sender.
  • Legal obligations: Information may be processed when necessary to comply with applicable law.

The GDPR recognizes consent and legitimate interests among the available legal bases for processing personal data. An organization relying on legitimate interests must consider whether the individual's rights override those interests.

Subscribers may withdraw consent at any time by using the unsubscribe link included in an email. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing performed before consent was withdrawn.

Depending on the services and settings used, Kit may process:

For additional information, review Kit's Privacy Policy and Kit's Data Processing Addendum.

6. Hosting Provider

The Site is hosted through Vercel. Vercel provides the infrastructure used to make the Site available and may process technical information on my behalf to deliver, secure, monitor, and troubleshoot the Site.

For additional information, review Vercel's Privacy Notice.

7. Cookies, Analytics, and Email Engagement

The Site does not currently use advertising cookies or cross-site behavioral advertising technology.

The Site also does not currently use an optional website-analytics service controlled by me. The hosting and newsletter providers may nevertheless process limited technical information necessary to provide, secure, and operate their respective services.

Emails delivered through Kit may include technology used to measure delivery and engagement, such as whether an email was opened or a link was selected. This information may be used to understand whether Letters from the Starlight are reaching and serving subscribers effectively.

If optional analytics, advertising pixels, or additional tracking technologies are added to the Site, this Privacy Policy will be updated and any legally required consent choices will be provided.

Retention periods may vary based on the type of information, the reason it was collected, and applicable legal requirements.
