About the Book

The Starlight was not always broken.

For generations, its power has lived beneath Verdant, breathing magic into the land and protecting its people. But when an attack threatens to place the Light in enemy hands, Avena Valehart makes a choice born of courage and desperation.

The Light answers by choosing her.

With the Starlight alive beneath her skin, Avena fears and resents the power she never wanted. Verdant demands the return of its Light. The Terranes have spilled blood to possess it. And if Avena remains, she risks being imprisoned by the very people she tried to save.

Her only path leads away from home.

Accompanied by Lara and Ryn, the friends who refuse to let her carry this burden alone, Avena follows the Light’s pull across lands scarred by its absence. Yet she cannot escape the warning of a mysterious Vespera man surrounded by living shadows:

“The Starlight has left the land and chosen you instead.
You will not survive it.”

To restore Verdant’s fading magic and prevent the Terranes from descending into war, Avena must return the Light to the place where it first touched the world. But fractured magic offers no promise of a merciful ending. Returning the Starlight could reconnect the broken lands and restore what has been lost.

Or it could break everything forever.

The Starlight wants to go home.

Avena may not survive taking it there.
